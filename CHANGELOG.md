# Changelog

## 1.0.33 (2026-11-19)
- Local Chat assistant: ask about your money and record transactions in plain sentences, offline.
- Optional on-device AI: 8 models matched to your device, resumable downloads, keep several models.
- Money keyboard with calculator and amount suggestions.
- Changing the main currency converts all amounts.
- New animations: sliding tabs, rolling numbers, Magic Lamp close effect on sheets.
- More complete English and Indonesian; smaller app; faster sync and startup.

## 1.0.32 (2026-11-17)
- Tabs on Home, Stats, and Accounts; voice input for notes; new primary colors.
- Review queue, spending pace, quick amount chips; unified pickers and swipe-to-close sheets.
- Faster logout and sync with progress; Android 15 edge-to-edge; accessibility fixes.
