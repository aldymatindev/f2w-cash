# Features

## Recording
- Expense, income, and transfer (with transfer fee) in seconds.
- Custom money keyboard with calculator and amount suggestions based on your habits.
- Categories and sub-categories (icon, emoji, color), split one payment across categories,
  bookmarks for frequent transactions, notes with voice input, receipt photo attachment.
- Record from a sentence in the Chat tab: _"lunch 25k yesterday with gopay"_.
- Receipt scanning (OCR) on the device: auto-crop, straighten, read total, date, merchant,
  and items; review before saving.
- Bank statement import (CSV/PDF) with duplicate detection.

## Accounts & money
- Cash, bank, e-wallet, debit and credit cards, investments; include or exclude from totals.
- Multiple currencies with live or manual exchange rates; changing the main currency
  converts every amount (with an automatic backup first).
- Balance reconciliation and adjustment history.

## Planning
- Monthly and yearly budgets with carry-over and envelope allocation.
- Savings goals: target date, auto-deposit, round-up, reminders, item photo.
- Loans (lend/borrow) with repayments and per-person view.
- Bills and recurring transactions with reminders.
- Saving challenges (no-spend days, weekly limits).
- Family profiles under one account.

## Insights
- Calendar, daily, monthly, and summary views.
- Statistics by category and account, monthly trend, spending pace and forecast,
  unusual-transaction detection, net-worth target.
- Monthly and yearly recap you can share as an image or PDF.

## Local finance assistant (Chat)
- Answers from your data on the device: totals by period, top categories, balances,
  budget left, latest transactions, "when did I last buy …", unusual spending.
- Understands English and Indonesian, tolerates typos, suggests what you may mean, and
  learns from your choices.
- Optional on-device language model (8 models, from 0.3 GB to 4.9 GB, suggested by your
  device RAM). Numbers always come from the app's own calculation; answers with invented
  numbers are rejected.

## Data & devices
- Local backup and restore (JSON/CSV); export CSV, PDF, Excel and share to any app.
- Optional Google Drive sync between your devices (your own Drive).
- PC Manager: view and edit data in a browser on the same device.
- Windows desktop app.

## Personalization
- Light/dark theme, primary colors, liquid-glass style, backgrounds, layout editor for
  Home/Stats/Accounts, navigation tabs, animation speed (respects "reduce motion").
- English and Indonesian.

## Free and Pro
**Free:** recording up to 50 transactions per month, default accounts and categories,
budgets, loans, bills, one active savings goal, basic statistics, local backup/restore,
the chat assistant, and on-device AI.

**Pro** (Google Play subscription): unlimited transactions, custom accounts, categories and
sub-categories, unlimited savings goals, advanced statistics, receipt photo and OCR,
Google Drive backup/restore and sync, CSV/PDF and PC exports.
